Welcome to České Budějovice

The capital of South Bohemia, founded in 1265 by King Přemysl Otakar II

History

Přemysl Otakar II Square

One of the largest squares in Europe, surrounded by impressive Baroque and Renaissance buildings. At its center stands the majestic Samson Fountain.

Architecture

Black Tower (Černá věž)

Climb the 225 steps of this 16th-century Renaissance tower and enjoy unmatched panoramic views of the city and its surroundings.

Excursion

Hluboká Castle

Only 10 km from the city, this neo-Gothic castle looks like something out of a fairy tale. A must-see excursion from České Budějovice.

Why Visit České Budějovice?

🏛️ History and Architecture

The city hosts one of the largest squares in Europe, surrounded by stunning Baroque and Renaissance buildings that tell centuries of history.

🍺 World-Renowned Beer

Home of the original Budweiser Budvar beer, a brewing tradition dating back to the city's founding in the 13th century.

🗺️ Ideal Starting Point

Perfect location to explore Český Krumlov (UNESCO Heritage), Hluboká Castle, Lipno Lake and Šumava National Park.
