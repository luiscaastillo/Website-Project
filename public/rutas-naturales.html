Practical Information

🚌 Transport

From České Budějovice there are regular train and bus connections to all destinations. RegioJet and České dráhy offer frequent services. A car is recommended for Šumava.

📅 Best Season

Spring and summer (May-September) for hiking and water activities. Autumn for forest colors. Winter for skiing in Lipno and Šumava.

🎒 Equipment

Bring hiking shoes, water, sun protection in summer and warm clothes at any time. In Šumava, bring a map or GPS as mobile coverage is limited.
