Hluboká nad Vltavou Castle

Distance: 10 km · Transport: Bus (20 min) or bicycle (40 min) · Visit duration: 2-3 hours

Hluboká Castle is considered one of the most beautiful castles in the Czech Republic. Its neo-Gothic style, inspired by Windsor Castle, features an impressive white facade surrounded by extensive English gardens.

The guided tour covers richly decorated halls with Flemish tapestries, porcelains, weapons and a library with thousands of volumes. The gardens are free to access and perfect for a quiet walk.

  • Main halls — Original 19th-century decoration with furniture, tapestries and paintings of great artistic value.
  • Aleš South Bohemia Gallery — Art museum located in the former stables of the castle.
  • Castle gardens — Extensive English-style gardens with centuries-old trees and views of the Vltava valley.

Český Krumlov — UNESCO World Heritage

Distance: 25 km · Transport: Train or bus (30-40 min) · Visit duration: Full day

Český Krumlov is a fairy-tale village declared UNESCO World Heritage. Its winding medieval streets and imposing castle, the second largest in the Czech Republic, will transport you to another era.

The Vltava river embraces the old town creating a postcard landscape. In summer you can go rafting on the river, and at any time of the year its galleries, restaurants and breweries offer an unforgettable experience.

  • Český Krumlov Castle — Monumental complex with a unique rotating Baroque theater in its gardens.
  • Historic center — Labyrinth of cobbled streets with houses painted in Renaissance style.
  • Vltava river — Walk along the river or rafting activities in summer.

Lipno Lake — The Sea of Bohemia

Distance: 55 km · Transport: Car or bus (1 hour) · Visit duration: Full day

The Lipno reservoir, known as the «Sea of Bohemia», is the largest artificial lake in the country. Surrounded by forests and mountains, it is an ideal destination both in summer and winter.

  • Treetop Walk (Stezka korunami stromů) — Wooden walkway 24 meters high offering spectacular views of the lake and the Austrian Alps.
  • Water sports — Sailing, windsurfing, kayaking and swimming at the reservoir's beaches during summer.
  • Cycling and hiking — Network of signposted trails around the lake, ideal for families.

Šumava National Park

Distance: 60-90 km · Transport: Car recommended · Duration: 1-2 days

Šumava (the Bohemian Forest) is the largest national park in the Czech Republic. A paradise for nature lovers, with dense spruce forests, glacial lakes, millennial peat bogs and unique fauna including lynxes and otters.

  • Černé jezero Lake (Black Lake) — The largest and deepest glacial lake in the Czech Republic, surrounded by primeval forests.
  • Schwarzenberg Canal — Historic wood transport canal, now a unique technical monument with parallel trails.
  • Hiking routes — Hundreds of kilometers of signposted trails for all levels, from family walks to mountain routes.

Practical Information

🚌 Transport

From České Budějovice there are regular train and bus connections to all destinations. RegioJet and České dráhy offer frequent services. A car is recommended for Šumava.

📅 Best Season

Spring and summer (May-September) for hiking and water activities. Autumn for forest colors. Winter for skiing in Lipno and Šumava.

🎒 Equipment

Bring hiking shoes, water, sun protection in summer and warm clothes at any time. In Šumava, bring a map or GPS as mobile coverage is limited.
