Practical Information

Location
České Budějovice, Jihočeský kraj, Czech Republic
How to Get There
Train or bus from Prague (approx. 2.5 hours). Frequent connections with České dráhy, RegioJet and FlixBus.
Best Time to Visit
May to September for outdoor activities. Autumn for incredible landscapes. Winter for skiing in Lipno.
Tips
Don't forget to try the original Budweiser Budvar beer. Wear comfortable shoes for the cobblestone streets. Book guided tours in advance during high season.
