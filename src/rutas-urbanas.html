Urban Routes

Walks through the historic center of České Budějovice

Routes Through the Heart of the City

Three itineraries to discover the treasures of the historic center

Route 1: The Grandeur of the Square

Approximate duration: 2 hours · Difficulty: Easy · Distance: 2.5 km

  • Přemysl Otakar II Square — Start your tour in this impressive square, one of the largest in Europe (133 × 133 meters). Admire the Baroque and Renaissance buildings surrounding it.
  • Samson Fountain (Samsonova kašna) — In the center of the square, this 18th-century Baroque fountain is one of the most photographed symbols of the city.
  • Town Hall (Radnice) — The majestic Baroque Town Hall building, with its decorated facade and three towers, dominates one side of the square.
  • St. Nicholas Cathedral (Katedrála sv. Mikuláše) — A few steps from the square, this imposing Baroque cathedral houses valuable frescoes and an impressive organ.
  • Black Tower (Černá věž) — Climb the 225 steps of this 16th-century hola for unmatched panoramic views of the city.

Route 2: Medieval Charm

Approximate duration: 1.5 hours · Difficulty: Easy · Distance: 2 km

  • Dominican Monastery — Discover this oasis of peace with its 13th-century Gothic church and beautiful medieval frescoes.
  • Iron Maiden Tower (Železná panna) — One of the best-preserved towers of the old medieval fortification of the city.
  • Panská and Česká Streets — Get lost in these charming historic streets with Renaissance buildings, craft shops and cozy cafes.
  • Malše River — End the walk by the river, where you can enjoy calm views and picturesque bridges.

Route 3: The Beer Trail

Approximate duration: 3 hours (with guided tour) · Difficulty: Easy · Distance: 3 km

  • Budweiser Budvar Brewery — Guided tour of the legendary brewery where the authentic original Budweiser is made. Fresh beer tasting included.
  • Masné krámy — The old medieval meat market, now converted into a famous restaurant where you can taste traditional Czech dishes with craft beer.
  • Return through the square — Enjoy one last beer on one of the square's terraces while admiring the sunset over the Baroque rooftops.

Practical Tips

🕐 Best Time

Early mornings are ideal to photograph the square without crowds. The Black Tower is open from 10:00 to 18:00 in high season.

👟 Footwear

The historic center has cobblestone streets. Wear comfortable footwear with good soles to enjoy the walks without discomfort.

📷 Photography

Best spots for photos: the top of the Black Tower, the Samson Fountain at sunset, and the arcades of the square with perspective.
